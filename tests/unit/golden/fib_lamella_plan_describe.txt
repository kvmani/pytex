Lamella for zone axis <011> in nickel, grain 7 (GOS 0.40 deg) at scan (12.50, 30.00) um. Of the 12 members of the orbit (every symmetry equivalent, both senses), [1 -1 0] lies closest to the surface plane, eps* = 3.21 deg out of it; that angle is the residual tilt the TEM holder must supply, because a vertically milled lamella can only put in-plane directions on the beam. Cut with the lamella normal n_L at theta_S = 33.83 deg in the sample frame (from X_s towards Y_s; long axis t_L at 123.83 deg), theta_I = 33.83 deg in the SEM image, and pattern rotation theta_ion = 33.83 deg for the normal (123.83 deg for the long axis). UNCALIBRATED: theta_ion uses the placeholder s_R = +1, R0 = 0 deg. Its sign and offset are instrument-specific and have not been measured; run the fiducial calibration protocol before milling, or the lamella may be cut at the wrong azimuth. TEM: double tilt, +/-30 deg x +/-30 deg (default); rectangular envelope alpha in [-30.0, +30.0] deg, beta in [-30.0, +30.0] deg. The in-plane mounting rotation phi and the front/back flip are unknown until the lamella is on the holder, so the residual is a known angle whose split into alpha and beta is not; roughly alpha ~ eps cos phi and beta ~ eps sin phi (a small-angle picture only - every number here comes from the exact solver). GUARANTEED: at the upper bound eps* + U the target stays inside the holder envelope with a 5 deg margin for every mounting rotation. Cross-check: PyTex's TEM navigation solver finds a least tilt of 3.2115 deg for the mounted lamella, against eps* = 3.2115 deg. The flip reverses the beam sense (the back branch looks down the reverse of the front's axis); for kinematic and centrosymmetric conditions that is immaterial, for CBED of a polar or non-centrosymmetric phase it is not. Uncertainty budget (standard uncertainties, combined in quadrature with unit sensitivity): EBSD orientation accuracy 0.50 deg (user input); intragranular spread 0.40 deg (grain orientation spread (GOS) from the map); registration residual 0.00 deg (declared, exact); stage and mount repeatability 2.00 deg (user input). Combined u(eps*) = 2.10 deg; expanded U = 2 u = 4.20 deg, so eps* = 3.21 +/- 4.20 deg and the verdict is judged on the upper bound 7.41 deg. Risks: [critical] The FIB pattern rotation uses an uncalibrated sign and offset; calibrate with a fiducial before milling. [info] The grain is assumed columnar: its orientation at the surface is taken to hold over the milling depth. EBSD sees only the top tens of nanometres, so a grain boundary below the surface would put a different crystal in the lamella. [info] The EBSD-to-FIB-to-TEM chain has been validated analytically and against PyTex's TEM tilt solver, but not yet against a lamella cut on a real instrument with its achieved TEM tilt recorded; treat the azimuths as a plan to check, not as a guarantee. Surface: the outward normal Z_s is +z of the EBSD specimen frame, X_s is its +x, Y_s = Z_s x X_s; scan rows increase along +y of the specimen frame, so a scan y coordinate maps to Y_s with factor +1. Registration: identity (decision D3) - image x along scan x, image y along scan y, one image unit per micrometre, no flip.
